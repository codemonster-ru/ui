# Icon Migration Batch 17

Status: approved by the project owner on 2026-08-01.

## Scope

- `creditCard`: replace the split solid mass with one rounded card, a stripe, and restrained payment
  details.
- `wallet`: replace the nested solid silhouette with a rounded wallet and a clear clasp pocket.
- `receipt`: replace the dense solid slip with symmetric torn edges and three content lines.

All icons use the canonical 24-unit canvas, 2-unit stroke, round terminals, and round joins. Their
public names and semantics are unchanged. The approved reference set and migration batches 02–16
remain frozen and are not modified by this batch.

## Review route

Open the playground at `/icons` and select `Batch 17 review` to compare the preserved old baseline,
approved geometry, and overlay samples at 16, 20, 24, and 32 px.
