# Section contract

Status: Active

Component: `Section`

Razor tag: `cm-section`

Section adds shared content inset. `surface=true` adds the shared background, border, radius, and
shadow treatment. `element` is `section`, `div`, `article`, or `aside` and defaults to `section`.
Consumers own headings and accessible landmark names. Root attributes and classes reach the selected
element. The trusted default slot is not interpreted, and Section adds no behavior.
